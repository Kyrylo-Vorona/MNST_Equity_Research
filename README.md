# MNST Equity Research

Educational equity research on Monster Beverage (MNST). Not investment advice.

**Recommendation:** Sell, target price $34 (downside ~25% vs. $45.34).

## Files
- `MNST_Equity_Research.pdf` — research report

## Notes
- DCF valuation date: December 31, 2025 (not rolled forward to the present).
- Comps based on the Q3 2026 10-Q balance sheet.
- Model structure follows the methodology of Pignataro.
